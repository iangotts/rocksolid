The Band

A love letter to the women who shaped rock.

RockChix is a Bay Area-based rock cover band with one mission: to bring the unfiltered swagger of female rock pioneers to every stage we step on — from festival main stages to wedding dance floors.

We pull from five decades of powerhouse women — Joan Jett, Patti Smith, Heart, Blondie, the Pretenders, Pink, Paramore, Avril Lavigne — alongside the canon of rock anthems everybody knows the words to. Our front line punches above its weight, our rhythm section locks like a vault, and we don't phone in a single song.

Whether you're booking a 5,000-person outdoor festival or a 75-person backyard wedding, you're getting the same thing: a band that shows up early, sounds enormous, and makes your event feel like the best night of the year.
