The Band

A love letter to the women who shaped rock.

Rock Solid is the Bay Area's female-fronted rock cover band — pulling from five decades of powerhouse women alongside the canon of rock anthems everybody knows the words to.

Joan Jett, Patti Smith, Heart, Blondie, the Pretenders, Pink, Paramore, Avril Lavigne — every song chosen to make a room move. Our front line punches above its weight, our rhythm section locks like a vault, and we don't phone in a single song.

Whether you're booking a 5,000-person festival or a 75-person backyard wedding, you're getting the same band: rehearsed until it's effortless, organized to the minute, easy to work with. You'll love the energy, the performance, and the delivery. And, oh, that songlist.
